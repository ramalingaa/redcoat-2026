PHASE 01 Build 1–3 days (AI-assisted) +
  • Define niche precisely — exact user, pain point, country
  • Identify incumbent — what do they currently use or pay for
  • Define the ONE differentiator (not 5, just 1)
  • Fork closest existing codebase (e.g. KidLearn for ESL variants)
  • Swap content, word banks, UI copy, color theme
  • Test touch on real iPhone + Android device
  • Set up RevenueCat — 3-day free trial → $9.99/mo or $49.99/yr
  • Wrap with Capacitor for native iOS + Android
PHASE 02 ASO — App Store Optimisation 2–3 hours +
  • Title: [Primary Keyword] — [Benefit] · max 30 chars
  • Subtitle: second most important keyword · 30 chars
  • Keyword field: 100 chars, comma-separated, medium volume + low competition
  • Description: first 3 lines lead with the pain — visible without tap
  • 5 screenshots per device (iPhone 6.7", iPad 12.9")
  • AI-generated app icon (1024×1024)
  • Trigger SKStoreReviewRequest after 3rd session
PHASE 03 Creator Outreach 20 DMs/week per app · ongoing +
  • Target: micro-influencers 5K–100K, engagement >3%, niche-specific
  • Find via hashtag search on Instagram + TikTok
  • Offer: free lifetime access + 30% affiliate on first month
  • Expect 10–15% response rate · 2–3 will post · 50–200 installs per post
  • Track all outreach in the Creators tab
PHASE 04 Acquire & Measure Weekly review every Monday +
  • Track: installs · trial starts · trial→paid conversion (target >25%) · MRR · churn
  • CAC target: under $10 · LTV target: over $50 · LTV:CAC ratio > 3:1
  • Tools: RevenueCat + App Store Connect Analytics (all free)
Decision Rules
20 subs in month 1 → continue investing
50 subs in month 2 → double down, pause others
<10 subs after 6 weeks → diagnose (ASO? audience? pricing?) → pivot or kill
PHASE 05 Double Down When app hits 50+ subscribers +
  • Expand content — v2 with more words/features
  • Add AI premium tier at $14.99/month
  • Run paid UA on Meta/TikTok — $5/day to start
  • Localise for 1 additional language/market
  • Reach out to 50 more creators
  • PR: pitch niche blogs, podcasts, newsletters
