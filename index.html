// THE LOOP

Research → Build →
ASO → Creators → MRR

PHASE 00Research — find the gap first1–2 hrs per idea+
  • Find 3–5 apps making money in the niche (App Store search)
  • Read ALL 1-star and 3-star reviews — that's the product roadmap
  • Confirm no free dominant alternative
  • Identify creator acquisition hashtag community
  • 5/5 criteria pass → build. Otherwise → kill and move on.
PHASE 01Build — fix the gap, nothing moreHours to 3 days (AI)+
  • Define ONE differentiator from competitor gap — not five
  • Fork closest codebase where possible
  • RevenueCat — 3-day free trial → $9.99/mo or $49.99/yr
  • Test in browser first. iOS/Android after web verified.
  • No features not in the gap analysis — scope creep kills speed
PHASE 02ASO — App Store Optimisation2–3 hrs+
  • Title: [Primary Keyword] — [Benefit] · max 30 chars
  • Keyword field: 100 chars, medium volume + low competition
  • Description: first 3 lines lead with the pain
  • 5 screenshots per device size (iPhone 6.7", iPad 12.9")
  • Review request after 3rd session (SKStoreReviewRequest)
PHASE 03Creator Outreach20 DMs/week per app+
  • Micro-influencers 5K–100K, engagement >3%, niche-specific
  • Free lifetime access + 30% affiliate on first month
  • 10–15% response · 2–3 posts · 50–200 installs per post
PHASE 04Measure & DecideEvery Monday · 30 min+
  • 20 subs month 1 → continue · 50 subs month 2 → double down
  • Under 10 after 6 weeks → diagnose or kill
  • RevenueCat (MRR) + App Store Connect (installs) — both free
