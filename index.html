PHASE 01 Build 1–3 days (AI-assisted) +
  • Define the niche precisely — who is the exact user, what pain, what country
  • Identify incumbent — what do they currently use or pay for
  • Define the ONE differentiator (not 5, just 1)
  • Fork closest existing codebase (e.g. KidLearn for ESL variants)
  • Swap content, word banks, UI copy, color theme
  • Test touch interactions on real iPhone + Android device
  • Set up RevenueCat — 3-day free trial → $9.99/month or $49.99/year
  • Wrap with Capacitor for native iOS + Android (npx cap init → sync → build)
PHASE 02 ASO — App Store Optimisation 2–3 hours +
  • Title: [Primary Keyword] — [Benefit] (max 30 chars)
  • Subtitle: Second most important keyword (30 chars)
  • Keyword field: 100 chars, comma-separated, medium volume + low competition
  • Description: First 3 lines lead with the pain — shown without "read more"
  • 5 screenshots per device size (iPhone 6.7", iPad 12.9")
  • AI-generated app icon (1024x1024)
  • Category: Education (primary)
  • Set up SKStoreReviewRequest to trigger after 3rd session
PHASE 03 Creator Outreach Ongoing — 20 DMs/week per app +
  • Target: micro-influencers 5K–100K, engagement >3%, niche-specific
  • Find via hashtag search on Instagram + TikTok
  • Offer: free lifetime access + 30% affiliate on first month per referral
  • Send 20 DMs per week per app (expect 10–15% response, 2–3 posts)
  • 1 good post from 50K niche creator = 50–200 installs
  • Track all outreach in Creators tab of this dashboard
PHASE 04 Acquire & Measure Weekly review — every Monday +
  • Track: Installs, Trial starts, Trial→Paid conversion (target >25%), MRR, Churn
  • CAC target: under $10 (organic/creator channel)
  • LTV target: over $50 (5+ months at $10/month)
  • Tools: RevenueCat (subs) + App Store Connect Analytics (installs)
  • Decision: 20 subs month 1 → continue · 50 subs month 2 → double down · under 10 after 6 weeks → diagnose or kill
PHASE 05 Double Down When app hits 50+ subscribers +
  • Expand content (v2 — more words/features)
  • Add AI premium tier at $14.99/month
  • Run paid UA on Meta/TikTok — $5/day to start
  • Localise for 1 additional language/market
  • Reach out to 50 more creators
  • PR: pitch niche blogs, podcasts, newsletters
