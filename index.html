// THE STRATEGY

Competitors tested
the waters for us.

The core insight
Don't build from the ground up. Find apps already making money in niche markets, study what's working, find the gap in 1-star and 3-star reviews, build a sharper focused version faster. Competitors already validated the market — no need to repeat that. Pick emerging proven ideas, adopt and improve.
How we pick an idea
1. Proven paying market — competitor is making real money (not just downloads)
2. Bad reviews reveal the gap — 1-star and 3-star reviews are the roadmap
3. No free dominant alternative — Wave kills invoice apps, GHIN kills golf apps
4. Recurring daily need — retention requires a habit, not a one-time event
5. Creator acquisition path — a niche community exists on Instagram or TikTok
6. Fast build — hours to days with AI assistance, not weeks
What we killed and why
Stoic Journal — market validated, but gap gone (4M users, Apple award winner)
Bar Exam — content moat not UX moat. Can't build the legal library.
Box Breathing — real buyer is institutions, not consumers. Wrong model.
Golf — GHIN is free and official. Can't beat a government system.
Invoice/Tax — Wave is free. Financial trust takes years. Wrong terrain.
Ramadan/Wedding — seasonal or one-time. Can't build MRR on them.
Nurse Shift — gap narrowing fast. Moved to watch list.
Carnivore — parked. Food database is a real scope problem. Revisit post-KidLearn.

// THE LOOP

Research → Build →
ASO → Creators → MRR

PHASE 00 Research — find the gap first 1–2 hours per idea +
  • Find 3–5 apps already making money in the niche (App Store search, Sensor Tower free tier)
  • Read ALL 1-star and 3-star reviews — that's the product roadmap
  • Check download trajectory — growing means expanding market, not saturated
  • Confirm no free dominant alternative exists
  • Identify creator acquisition path — which hashtag community exists?
  • Only proceed if 5/5 criteria pass. If not, kill and move to next idea.
PHASE 01 Build — fix the complaints, nothing more Hours to 3 days (AI-assisted) +
  • Define ONE differentiator from competitor gap — not five
  • Fork closest existing codebase where possible
  • Set up RevenueCat — 3-day free trial → $9.99/mo or $49.99/yr
  • Test in browser first — iOS/Android packaging after web is verified
  • Wrap with Capacitor for native (npx cap add ios / android)
  • Do not add features not in the competitor gap analysis — scope creep kills speed
PHASE 02 ASO — App Store Optimisation 2–3 hours +
  • Title: [Primary Keyword] — [Benefit] · max 30 chars
  • Subtitle: second most important keyword · 30 chars
  • Keywords: 100 chars, medium volume + low competition
  • Description: first 3 lines lead with the pain — visible without tap
  • 5 screenshots per device (iPhone 6.7", iPad 12.9")
  • AI-generated app icon (1024×1024)
  • Trigger review request after 3rd session (SKStoreReviewRequest)
PHASE 03 Creator Outreach 20 DMs/week per app · ongoing +
  • Target: micro-influencers 5K–100K, engagement >3%, niche-specific
  • Find via hashtag search on Instagram + TikTok
  • Offer: free lifetime access + 30% affiliate on first month
  • Expect 10–15% response rate · 2–3 will post · 50–200 installs per post
  • Track all outreach in Creators tab
PHASE 04 Measure & Decide Every Monday · 30 min +
  • 20 subs in month 1 → continue investing
  • 50 subs in month 2 → double down, pause others
  • Under 10 after 6 weeks → diagnose (ASO? audience? pricing?) → fix or kill
  • Tools: RevenueCat (MRR) + App Store Connect (installs) — both free
  • LTV target: $50+ · CAC target: under $10 · LTV:CAC ratio > 3:1
PHASE 05 Double Down — when app hits 50+ subscribers Winner takes more resources +
  • Expand content / features (v2)
  • Add AI premium tier at $14.99/month
  • Paid UA on Meta/TikTok — $5/day to start
  • Localise for 1 additional language/market
  • Reach out to 50 more creators
  • PR: pitch niche blogs, podcasts, newsletters
The Loop v2.0 — Research first, build second
